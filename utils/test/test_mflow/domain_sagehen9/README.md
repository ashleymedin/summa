# domain_sagehen9 — one HRU per MODFLOW cell, nine GRUs, coupled to MODFLOW 6

Generated by [`../tools/build_sagehen9.py`](../tools/build_sagehen9.py) from the
`ex-gwf-sagehen` grid alone. Regenerate it with

    ../tools/build_sagehen9.py

Every active cell of the 73 x 81, 90 m grid is one SUMMA HRU, so **HRU area equals
cell area by construction** and `hru2cell_map.txt` is the identity map. The 3387
cells fall into the 9 subcatchments of the channel network that D8 over `DIS/TOP`
produces, one GRU each, and each GRU carries a stream HRU for its reach.

    GRU      1     2     3     4     5     6     7     8     9
    HRUs   721   456   393   604   446   295   103   340    29
    km2   5.84  3.69  3.18  4.89  3.61  2.39  0.83  2.75  0.23
    reach    1     2     3     4     5     6     7     8     9
    drains   4     4     6     6     7     7     9     9   out

`downHRUindex` is the D8 receiver. Where a flow path leaves its GRU — exactly one
HRU per GRU, the foot of its reach — it drains to that GRU's stream HRU, which
`run_oneGRU` treats as a GRU outlet. Water then moves between GRUs through the
reaches, in `topology.nc`, not through the HRU cascade.

## What it is for

The 1:1 map removes two artefacts of the coarse cases at once:

- **no aggregation error.** One HRU per cell means the clipped groundwater-ET ramp
  is evaluated on a single cell's water table, so `f(mean psi)` and `mean f(psi)`
  coincide. The per-cell factor the coupler computes becomes a consistency check
  on `scalarTranspireLimAqfr` rather than a correction.
- **no area mismatch.** `domain_sagehen1` carries 28.94 km² against the grid's
  27.435 km² (5.19% out) and the 9-HRU delineation 29.56 km² (7.1%). Here the two
  agree to round-off, so the coupled water budget's mapping residual is the only
  thing left in it.

It also makes lateral routing SUMMA's: `modLatflow` along `downHRUindex`, then
mizuRoute over the 9 reaches, with no MODFLOW MVR.

## Construction

Flow directions come from a priority-flooded `TOP` — the raw field holds one
interior pit, at row 27 column 72 — but every reported `elevation` is the raw
`DIS/TOP`. The steepest-descent scan order is that of
`determine_runoff_conns_4mvr` in the upstream `ex-gwf-sagehen.py`, so the cascade
follows the same paths the original model's MVR did.

The channel network is the cells with 250 or more cells above them, the threshold
at which the network has 5 heads and 4 junctions, hence 9 links. That reproduces
the reach topology of the independent Copernicus 9-HRU delineation of the same
basin exactly, and its area distribution to within a few percent per subcatchment
— including its 0.23 km² outlet reach.

A reach's plan area is `length x width` with mizuRoute's `wscale`, taken out of
the channel cells it runs through, so the GRU area stays the cell area. A stream
HRU maps to those same cells with equal weight, which is close to the areal share
the reach takes from each.

The HRUs are written **GRU by GRU, each GRU's stream HRU last**. `hru_index` in
`hru2cell_map.txt` is SUMMA's internal index, which runs GRU by GRU, so only that
order makes the identity map line up; a file ordered by cell instead sends every
HRU past the first GRU to the wrong cell, which `check_hru_elevation` catches.

Forcing is `domain_sagehen1`'s single-HRU series given to every HRU. The source
carries no sub-GRU information, so there is nothing finer to distribute; the
domain tests structure, not a new forcing product.

## Expected at start-up

`check_hru_area` reports 0% out. One HRU reports draining to a higher HRU — the
breached pit at row 27 column 72, where every neighbour is above the cell, which
is what a pit is. Nine GRUs each report one HRU draining out of the GRU.

## Caveat

The grid has no georeference. `latitude`/`longitude` come from fitting the `TOP`
valley line to the Copernicus mainstem: east-west is pinned by the outlet cell,
north-south only to about two cells. That reaches nothing but the solar geometry,
where 200 m of latitude is 0.002°.

## Running

    ../run_sagehen9.sh

Needs `bin/summa_modflow6.exe`, built with `-DUSE_MODFLOW6=ON`.
